    Spacebar Clicker is a free game that runs in the browser. Press the spacebar, or tap the SPACE key, to earn points. Spend points on helpers that keep scoring while you rest. Your progress stays on this device. The leaderboard is shared by everyone who plays.

    How to play

    1. Press the spacebar, or click the SPACE key. Each press adds your current tap power to the score.
    2. The line under the score shows points per second from helpers, your tap power after upgrades, and burst: the most presses you landed in one second.
    3. Ten presses open the shop. The next helper appears after you buy the one before it.
    4. Some helpers add the same amount every second. Growing helpers add more with every copy. Spring Kit doubles the points from your own presses.
    5. Open Top, enter a nickname of at least two characters, and save. The board keeps the best 20 names from every player.
    6. Reset progress clears the current score and the helpers you bought. Names already on the board stay. Fullscreen expands the game. Sound turns the click tone off, and that choice is remembered here.
